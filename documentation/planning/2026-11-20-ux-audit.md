# UX audit, 20–21 Nov 2026 (overnight)

**Status:** finished 20 Nov 2026 (night). For Ben to review on Monday.
**Plan:** [UX and brand plan](../plans/UX_BRAND_PLAN.md), step 1.
**Where:** live test site `https://erp-live.pages.dev`, company "Demo Cosmetics Factory" (test data only).
**How:** Claude looked at each screen in a browser. **Read only:** no approving, posting, saving or deleting.
Screenshots are not kept in the repo.

> **In short**
> - **About 150 findings on about 45 screens.** Most are about clutter, words and consistency. A few are real bugs.
> - **The worst for a demo:** the invoice page has no Print or PDF button; the demo company shows a bank balance of minus 3.8 million,
>   hundreds of days of unpaid bills, test items and real people's emails; notifications are walls of raw numbers.
> - **Real bugs:** long lists stop at 200–500 rows with no way to see older rows; an expired batch shows "Available"; the Prices page
>   layout overlaps; date boxes ignore the company date format; the invoice history shows "minor units".
> - **Arabic:** the layout is correct, but units, warehouse names and item names stay English.
> - **Biggest single win:** fix the demo data. Many "this looks broken" moments come from the data, not the code.

**Severity**
- **P1:** broken, wrong, or a buyer notices it in the first minutes.
- **P2:** confusing or crowded. It slows people down.
- **P3:** polish.

## Findings by screen

- **P1** 4 of 6 cards say "Action required" in red. When everything is urgent, nothing is urgent.
- **P1** "Top products" and "Receivables and payables" both open `/sales/invoices` (found in code; see the UX plan, quick fixes).
- **P2** The home page has two jobs: summary cards at the top, and a launcher of 13 apps below. The launcher repeats the "Apps" menu.
- **P2** Card subtitles repeat the title in other words ("Profit and loss" / "What is left from sales after…"). The cards are text-heavy.
- **P2** The bottom note ("As of … not official financial statements") is small, grey and long.
- **P3** App icons are not consistent: most are icons, but Accounting and Treasury show the letters "A" and "T".
- **P3** The top bar shows only "Apps" and the company name. There is no product name or logo.

### Inventory overview (`/inventory`)
- **P2** The side menu has **17 items in 4 groups** (Do, Stock, History, Settings and planning). This is one module only.
- **P2** "Do" actions show two times: in the side menu and as 5 big cards. The cards are good; the menu copy is extra.
- **P2** 5 action cards in a 4-column grid leave one card alone on a second row.

## Arabic (right-to-left) check

Checked: Home, Inventory overview, Stock on hand, a sales invoice, and the menus. Language set to Arabic for this check, then set
back (see the end of this report).

- Good: the layout turns right-to-left correctly. Menus, cards, tables and buttons sit on the correct side. Most labels are translated.
- **P1** **Units are not translated:** "piece", "litre" show in English in Arabic mode (Stock on hand).
- **P1** **Warehouse names stay English** ("Finished goods", "Cold room", "Quarantine"), also inside Arabic sentences:
  "OUT-0340 معتمد: اصرف المخزون من Finished goods". Warehouses need an Arabic name field, like items.
- **P1** **Item names stay English** in lists and on the invoice even when the item has an Arabic name. In Arabic mode, show the
  Arabic name (fall back to English when empty), and drop the separate "Arabic name" column.
- **P2** The invoice total rows ("قبل الضريبة", "ضريبة القيمة المضافة", "الإجمالي شامل الضريبة") use the **monospace number font**
  for Arabic words. The letters look broken and spaced out.
- **P2** Money is written "EGP 1,126,202" inside Arabic sentences. The mixed direction moves "EGP" and the full stop to odd places at
  line ends. In Arabic, write "1,126,202 ج.م" (or keep EGP after the number).
- **P2** Mixed English words in Arabic headers: "SKU", "اختر تنسيق SKU", "تصدير CSV".
- **P3** Charts run right-to-left, so the newest month is on the left. Many Arabic finance apps keep time left-to-right. Decide one rule.
- **P3** Demo company name, customers and suppliers are English only. An Arabic demo company would show the product better in Egypt.

## Patterns seen on many screens (fix once, in shared parts)

1. **Subtitles explain process rules** in long sentences. Use one short line, and a "?" tip for detail.
2. **Page titles differ from menu labels.**
3. **Links in tables:** some rows underlined, some not.
4. **Row actions** as several plain-text words or stacked buttons. Use one "…" menu, and a different look for "Remove".
5. **Buttons:** black, white, grey and full-width bars mixed. Use one primary button per screen.
6. **Wide tables** with empty columns and wrapped names.
7. **Plain dropdowns** for items next to type-to-search for customers and suppliers.
8. **List limits** (see top fix 3).
9. **Settings spread across modules** (approval policies, item groups, inventory settings, WhatsApp, language in the tutorial).
