Subject: ERP update 2: alerts, forecast, sign-up and tutorial built

Follow-up to this afternoon's update. Since then:

BUILT AND LIVE (deployed by the automatic pipeline; last run green)

- Alerts: rules tell the right people when stock is low, an item will run out within N days (from the company's own history), a batch is about to expire, a customer invoice is late, or the month is losing money. Teams (Purchasing, Warehouse, Management) decide who hears what. One digest message per person, not one per item.
- Stock forecast: days of stock left per item, supplier lead time from real past purchases, a suggested order quantity, and a label that says when the history is too thin to trust.
- Company-wide language (English or Arabic), set for the whole company in Settings.
- Sign-up: a new company creates an account, then sets up its company (name, language, currency, time zone) and becomes its owner with every app switched on.
- Getting-started tutorial: a guide with a live setup checklist, shown only in newly signed-up companies, never in the sample company.
- Fix found along the way: a brand-new company had no screen to create its first warehouse, so it could not add an item. Added.
- Profit overview page: opens from the "making or losing money" card, with a 12-month chart and a month-by-month table.
